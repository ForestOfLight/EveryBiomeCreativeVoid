A behavior pack that adds a void dimension for every biome. Add it to any world and teleport between biomes with [`/biometp`](#biometp-biome-string).

This is particularly useful for mobfarmers, who might want a large area of a certain biome to test their designs.

## Usage

Every Biome Creative's latest release can be downloaded from its [Releases Page](https://github.com/ForestOfLight/EveryBiomeCreativeVoid/releases) right here on GitHub. Open the `.mcpack` to import it into Minecraft, then add it to a world under **Behavior Packs**. It does not require experiments.

Updates are automatic, based on when Mojang adds new biomes to the game. Check the releases page when you want access to a new biome in your creative world!

#### **/biometp <biome: String>**

Teleports you between biome dimensions, or to a vanilla dimension. Your coordinates are kept during dimension change.
